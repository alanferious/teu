Drill

Spaced repetition. Say the story, the three numbers and the “but” out loud, then flip and grade yourself honestly. Containers you know come back less often.

Number recall

Each example's three key numbers, one at a time. Type the number that matches the description. Units don't matter, so “45” counts for “45%”, and answers within 1% are accepted.

Pick the example

In the exam you have to pick the right example fast. Each question names a concept; choose the case that illustrates it.

Write & check

Practise a Paper 1 (b)-style paragraph built on one example. The checker reads it for the parts examiners look for, then you place it in a markband yourself. Nothing leaves this page.
